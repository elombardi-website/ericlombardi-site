About Eric.

I work in financial services and technology, and founded More Neighbours Toronto in 2021. In 2026, I ran for leader of the Ontario Liberal Party. My writing and advocacy have focused on housing, economic growth, and the prospects of younger Canadians.

Founder and former president, More Neighbours Toronto (2021 to 2026). Former chair, Build Toronto (2025 to 2026). Ontario Liberal leadership candidate, 2026.

I was raised in the Greater Toronto Area and studied nanotechnology engineering at the University of Waterloo. I then built a career in technology and finance, including several years in San Francisco, before coming home during the pandemic to be closer to family and the communities that raised me.

Since then my consulting work has focused on strategy, acquisitions, product and delivery for financial services and technology companies, independently and, for larger engagements, with partner firms. You can read more on the consulting page.

In 2021 I founded More Neighbours Toronto to confront the housing crisis head on. It became the largest grassroots housing-abundance organization in Canada and a driving force in Toronto's zoning reform. In 2025 I co-founded and chaired Build Toronto, part of the Build Canada network. In 2026 I ran for leader of the Ontario Liberal Party on a platform of growth and government capability, and published a fully costed plan for the province. I withdrew in September 2026 and I'm no longer part of either organization, though I still cheer for both.

I write about Ontario's economy, housing, infrastructure and public institutions, here and in places like The Hub and the Toronto Star. The through-line is that Ontario should be a place where people can afford a home, build a career, raise a family in security, and trust that public institutions work for them.

I live in Toronto.
