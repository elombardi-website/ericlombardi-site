About

Finance and technology builder, turned civic builder.

I'm Eric Lombardi. I work on hard problems for companies in financial services and technology, and I spend the rest of my time arguing, writing and organizing for an Ontario that grows again.

I was raised in the Greater Toronto Area and studied nanotechnology engineering at the University of Waterloo. I then built a career in technology and finance, starting in consulting at Capco, then in San Francisco as chief of staff at Cut+Dry, a venture-backed B2B fintech, before coming home during the pandemic to be closer to family and the communities that raised me.

Since then I've worked on some of the bigger decisions in Canadian financial services: the strategy and sale of a digital-banking business, the modernization of a payments platform serving credit unions across two provinces, and the case for a global bank to bring real-time payments in-house. I work independently and, for larger engagements, with partner firms. You can read more on the consulting page.

In 2021 I founded More Neighbours Toronto to confront the housing crisis head on. It became the largest grassroots housing-abundance organization in Canada and a driving force in Toronto's zoning reform. In 2025 I co-founded and chaired Build Toronto, part of the Build Canada network. In 2026 I ran for leader of the Ontario Liberal Party on a platform of growth and government capability, and published a fully costed plan for the province. I withdrew in September 2026 and I'm no longer part of either organization, though I still cheer for both.

I write about Ontario's economy, housing, infrastructure and public institutions, here and in places like The Hub and the Toronto Star. The through-line in all of it is the same: Ontario should be a place where people can afford a home, build a career, raise a family in security, and trust that public institutions work for them.

I live in Toronto.
