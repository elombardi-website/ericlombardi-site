How I work

From a conversation to a full program.

Engagements scale to the problem. Some need one person for two weeks, and some need a team for a year.

1

A conversation

Thirty minutes, no charge. Tell me the problem and I'll tell you honestly whether I'm the right person and what it would take.

2

A focused engagement

Two to eight weeks for a decision memo, a business case, a diligence read or an options assessment. One senior person, direct access, a clear answer.

3

A program

Months, not weeks, from strategy through delivery, with a team sized to the work. Design, engineering, data and change management alongside the strategy.

Partners

Working with Electric Mind

For larger work I draw on partnerships with firms like Electric Mind, a Toronto consulting and engineering firm I work with. Electric Mind describes itself as a forward-deployed AI firm: strategists, designers and engineers who take a problem from strategy through to systems running in production, with deep experience in financial services.

That lets me scale from one person to a full delivery team without changing the way we work together. A small engagement can grow into a complex one without a handoff, and a complex one can start with a single conversation.
