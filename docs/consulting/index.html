Consulting

Strategy and delivery for the tough decisions.

I help executives, boards, founders and investors in financial services and technology decide what to build, buy or sell, and then get it delivered. Working with Electric Mind lets me take a problem from concept to strategy to delivery, with mature partners who know how to do it.

Email me

Most of my work starts with a question that has a lot of money, time or reputation attached to it. Should we bring this platform in-house or re-contract? Is this business worth more to us or to someone else? What would it actually take to ship this, and who do we need? I do the thinking, build the case, and stay to make it real.

I've spent nine years in payments, digital banking, embedded finance and venture-backed fintech, on both the operating and advisory sides. That gives me the forensic detail to get the numbers right and the judgment to know which numbers matter. And because I work with Electric Mind, a Toronto consulting and engineering firm, I can bring a full team of strategists, designers, engineers and data specialists to the table when the work calls for it.

What I do

Four kinds of problems.

Strategy and big problems

The thinking before the money moves.

  • Business cases and build-versus-buy decisions
  • Vendor, platform and partnership strategy
  • Operating models and organization design
  • Market entry, product strategy and roadmaps
  • Executive and board decision materials

For example: Built the economic case for a global bank to move real-time peer-to-peer payments in-house, framing the modernize, re-contract and build options for executive decision.

Mergers, acquisitions and divestitures

Buying, selling and integrating businesses and products.

  • Options assessments: grow, partner or sell
  • Commercial and product due diligence
  • Carve-outs and transition planning
  • Post-merger integration and cut-over
  • Management presentations and data rooms

For example: Led the due-diligence workstream and oversaw the carve-out and sale of Central 1's Forge digital-banking business to Intellect Design. Earlier, led a nine-person team through the zero-defect post-merger integration of an acquired fintech product.

Ventures, startups and advisory

For founders and investors building something new.

  • Fundraising materials and financial models
  • Product strategy, MVP scope and go-to-market
  • Strategic advisor and board-adjacent roles
  • Diligence support for investors
  • Chief-of-staff style operating support

For example: Partnered with the CEO of Cut+Dry, a San Francisco B2B fintech, to raise more than US$25 million across two rounds, and returned in 2025 to advise on its Series B. Laid the product foundation for Atelio, FIS's embedded-finance platform.

Delivery

I stay through delivery, not just the deck.

  • Program design and governance
  • Pod-based delivery models and team design
  • Estimation, resourcing and multi-year plans
  • Vendor and partner management
  • KPIs, OKRs and executive reporting

For example: Designed the four-pod delivery model, bottom-up resourcing plan and governance for a global bank's payments program, and managed a 14-person team launching a Canadian bank's digital platform on time and on budget.

How I work

From a conversation to a full program.

Engagements scale to the problem. Some need one person for two weeks, and some need a team for a year. I can do both.

1

A conversation

Thirty minutes, no charge. Tell me the problem and I'll tell you honestly whether I'm the right person and what it would take.

2

A focused engagement

Two to eight weeks for a decision memo, a business case, a diligence read or an options assessment. One senior person, direct access, a clear answer.

3

A program

Months, not weeks, from strategy through delivery, with a team sized to the work. Design, engineering, data and change management alongside the strategy.
