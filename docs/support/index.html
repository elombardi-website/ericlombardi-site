The pledge

My commitment on how the money is used

Everything raised here goes to the work and nothing goes to me. I earn my living as a consultant. Supporter funds are dedicated to building a community around the ideas I advocate for: convening people, running events, producing research and writing, and travelling to connect with audiences across Ontario and, where there's interest, across the country.

I'll publish a plain summary of what came in and what it was spent on, so anyone who contributes can see where it went.

This is not a political contribution and it is not a charitable donation. It is support for independent public policy work, and no tax receipt is issued.

Questions

The fine print, in plain words.

Is this a political donation?

No. This supports my independent public policy and advocacy work. It is not a contribution to any party, candidate or campaign.

Do I get a tax receipt?

No. This is not a registered charity and no tax receipt is issued.

Is there a minimum, or a set amount?

No. Contribute whatever you like, monthly or once.

How do I change or cancel a monthly contribution?

Any time, from the confirmation email or your account on the payment platform.

Where does the money go?

Community, events, travel, research and writing. None of it to me personally. I'll post a plain summary of income and spending.

Do I have to contribute to get the updates?

No. Signing up for updates is free and always will be.
