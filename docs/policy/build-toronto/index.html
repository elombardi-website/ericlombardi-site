Build Toronto

The Toronto chapter of Build Canada: founders, operators and civic leaders publishing concrete, ambitious proposals for the city on housing, infrastructure, governance and growth.

Build Toronto ↗Memos ↗
Role
Chair and co-founder
When
September 2025 to January 2026

Build Canada launched Build Toronto in September 2025 as a pilot for a new model of municipal civic engagement, and I chaired it from the start. The idea is practical: bring the people who have actually built things, founders, operators and builders, into the room with the people who write policy, and ship memos that a city government can adopt.

The chapter publishes memos by entrepreneurs and civic leaders on housing, infrastructure, governance and growth. My own contribution, in October 2025, argued that old Toronto must grow to relieve the suburbs. By the 2026 municipal election the chapter had published nearly twenty memos and built voter tools, including a candidate directory and a ward finder.

I stepped back in January 2026 to run for the Ontario Liberal leadership and I'm no longer involved day to day. The chapter continues without me, and the broader Build Canada network is linked here so you can follow and join it.
