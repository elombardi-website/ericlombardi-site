Public policy and advocacy

Public policy and civic work.

Through More Neighbours Toronto, Build Toronto, and my campaign for the Ontario Liberal leadership, I have worked on housing, economic growth, public services, and a better deal for younger Ontarians.

Six areas of advocacy

Housing affordability and supply

Make it easier to build enough homes for every stage of life: zoning reform, faster approvals, and more housing choices in existing neighbourhoods.

Economic growth and productivity

A more competitive economy, where businesses invest and grow, workers earn more, and people can build a prosperous life.

Infrastructure and transportation

Build transit and other infrastructure faster and at a reasonable cost, and fix how governments plan, procure and deliver major projects.

Government accountability and effectiveness

Challenge opaque decisions, waste and soft corruption, and push for public institutions that deliver results.

Stronger cities and municipal reform

Give municipalities the funding, authority and capacity to serve growing communities, starting with a new deal between Ontario and its cities.

Intergenerational fairness and opportunity

Restore younger Canadians' ability to afford a home, build a career, raise a family and gain financial security.
