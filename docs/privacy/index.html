Privacy

Privacy policy

This site collects as little as it can. Here is what it collects, why, and what you can do about it.

Last updated October 26, 2026

This policy explains how personal information is collected, used and protected through ericlombardi.ca (the "Site"), in accordance with Canadian privacy law, including the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), and Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL).

What is collected

Information you give me. If you subscribe, contact me, register for an event or support the work, you may provide your name, email address, postal code or city, and the content of any message. Postal codes are used only to understand where readers and supporters are, so I can plan events and travel.

Information collected automatically. If analytics are enabled, the Site uses a privacy-friendly service that does not use cookies and does not identify individuals, recording aggregate page views, referrers and device types. No advertising trackers are used.

Third-party platforms. Payments for supporters are handled by a third-party platform (such as Patreon), which has its own privacy policy. I never see your card details. Embedded content from X, YouTube or Spotify may set its own cookies when you interact with it.

How it is used

  • To send you writing, event invitations and updates you asked for.
  • To reply to messages and manage event registrations.
  • To understand, in aggregate, who reads this site and where they are.
  • To meet legal obligations.

Personal information is never sold or rented.

Consent and unsubscribing

Commercial electronic messages are sent only with your express consent, given when you subscribe. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and requests are honoured within ten business days as CASL requires. You can also withdraw consent any time through the contact form.

Sharing

Information is shared only with service providers that run the Site's email, analytics, event registration and payments, and only for those purposes, or where the law requires it.

Storage and security

Data is stored with reputable providers and protected with reasonable administrative and technical safeguards. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but I work to protect your information, and I keep it only as long as needed for the purposes above.

Your rights

You can ask what personal information I hold about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, or withdraw consent, through the contact form.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date at the top reflects the latest revision.
